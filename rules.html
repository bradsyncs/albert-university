CLAN RULES.

These standards help keep AU competitive, organized and respectful.

01

RESPECT

Treat every member, opponent and staff member with respect.

02

TEAMWORK

Work with your teammates and put the clan before individual arguments.

03

ACTIVITY

Stay active and communicate when you are unavailable.

04

COMPETE

Represent AU seriously and give your best effort during competition.

05

MATURITY

Keep conversations and behavior appropriate for the AU community.

06

STAFF

Follow reasonable instructions from AU staff and leadership.
