Albert University

Clan Rules

Every AU member is expected to understand and follow these standards.

01

Weekly REP Requirement

Members are expected to earn 2,500 REP each week unless they have approved time off.

02

Time-Off Tickets

If you cannot meet the required activity level, submit a time-off ticket so staff can review your situation.

03

Fair Play

Exploiting, cheating, abusing bugs, or intentionally ruining competitive experiences is not allowed.

04

Protect AU

Members are expected to protect AU's reputation, information, community, and overall standing.

05

Behavior & Chat

Treat members, staff, and other communities with respect. Harassment, excessive toxicity, and inappropriate behavior are not acceptable.
