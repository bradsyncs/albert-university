AU Athletic Association

Athletics

The AU Athletic Association gives members different paths to participate in the organization based on their competitive role and involvement.

Competitive

D1 Athlete

The highest athletic pathway within AU. D1 Athletes are expected to represent the organization at a high competitive level.

  • High-level competition
  • Strong activity expectations
  • Represent AU competitively
Development

D2 Athlete

A competitive development pathway for players looking to improve, compete, and work toward higher levels.

  • Competitive development
  • Skill improvement
  • Opportunity to progress
Community

Clan Member

Clan Members contribute to AU through community activity, clan participation, and supporting the organization.

  • Clan participation
  • Community involvement
  • Represent AU
