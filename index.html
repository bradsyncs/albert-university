AU CLAN RULES

KNOW THE STANDARD.

Every member is expected to understand and follow these rules.

01 REP
02 TIME OFF
03 FAIR PLAY
04 PROTECTION
05 BEHAVIOR
01

WEEKLY REP REQUIREMENT

Members are expected to maintain 2,500 weekly REP unless they have approved time off.

02

TIME-OFF TICKETS

If you cannot meet your activity requirements, submit a time-off ticket through the appropriate AU staff system.

03

FAIR PLAY

Do not exploit, cheat, abuse bugs or intentionally ruin competitive experiences.

04

CLAN PROTECTION

Members are expected to protect the reputation, information and community of Albert University.

05

BEHAVIOR & CHAT

Respect members, staff and other communities. Harassment, excessive toxicity and inappropriate behavior are not acceptable.
