Official Clan Rules

Activity Requirement

Daily REP & Activity

  • Members must gain a minimum of 2,500 REP weekly.
  • Members who require time off must open a formal time-off ticket prior to missing quota.
  • Missing weekly REP without an approved time-off ticket may result in immediate removal from the clan.
Integrity

Fair Play & No Boosting

  • Exploiting or cheating results in an instant, unappealable ban.
  • Strictly no alt farming or friend boosting.
  • Do not play against suspicious low-overall accounts to boost stats or REP.
  • If matched onto the opposing team of a fellow clan member, leave the game as soon as possible.
Security

Clan Protection

  • Watch out for individuals attempting to frame AU or get members reported.
  • Clip your POV immediately if you feel targeted, watched, or harassed.
  • Leave suspicious or non-compliant games immediately.
  • Keep track of approved member accounts and official records.
Conduct

Behavior & Chat

  • No toxicity under any circumstances.
  • Do not point fingers at teammates or opponents.
  • Do not talk down to or make fun of different clans. Stay humble and show good sportsmanship.
  • Do not spam text or voice chat channels.

Tryout Application

All accepted applicants begin at the TRIAL MEMBER starting rank. Placement into D1 or D2 is earned through evaluation.
